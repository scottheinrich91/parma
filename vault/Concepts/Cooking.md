# Fundamental Cooking Concepts

Mastering fundamental kitchen chemistry and physical techniques allows for consistent culinary execution.

## 1. Gluten Development

Gluten forms when water hydrates the proteins *glutenin* and *gliadin*, and mechanical energy aligns them into elastic sheets.
- **High Hydration**: Yields extensibility and open crumb structure.
- **Kneading**: Vital for dishes like [[Recipes/Pasta|Fresh Handmade Pasta]].

## 2. Emulsification & Sauce Building

An emulsion is a stable mixture of two immiscible liquids (such as oil and water) facilitated by an emulsifying agent like lecithin in egg yolks.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When finishing pasta sauces, always reserve starchy pasta water to bind fats and liquid into a glossy, velvety glaze.

## 3. Heat Management & Maillard Reaction

The Maillard reaction occurs between amino acids and reducing sugars at temperatures above 140°C (285°F), producing hundreds of complex flavor compounds and appealing browning.
