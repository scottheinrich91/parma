# Artisanal Fresh Pasta

Fresh handmade egg pasta offers a silky texture that elevates traditional Italian dishes like tagliatelle, pappardelle, and fettuccine.

> [!TIP]
> Use high-protein Tipo 00 flour and fresh pasture-raised eggs for a golden dough with optimal elasticity. See [[Concepts/Cooking#Gluten Development]] for details on kneading.

## Ingredients

- **Flour**: 300g Tipo 00 flour (or 200g 00 flour + 100g semolina rimacinata)
- **Eggs**: 3 large whole eggs + 1 yolk
- **Olive Oil**: 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
- **Salt**: 1/2 tsp fine sea salt

## Method

1. **The Well (Fontana)**: Mound flour onto a clean wooden surface. Form a deep crater in the center.
2. **Emulsification**: Crack eggs into the center, add salt and oil. Whisk with a fork, gradually incorporating flour from the inner walls.
3. **Kneading**: Once dough comes together, knead vigorously for 8–10 minutes until smooth and springy.
4. **Resting**: Wrap tightly in cling wrap and rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes.
5. **Rolling & Cutting**: Roll through a pasta machine down to setting 6 or 7, dusting lightly with semolina. Slice into ribbons.
