# Espresso Machine Maintenance

Routine cleaning guarantees peak extraction pressure and prevents mineral buildup in internal thermocoils.

## Cleaning Checklist

- [ ] Backflush group head with blind basket (Weekly)
- [ ] Soak portafilter and steam wand tip in Cafiza solution (Bi-weekly)
- [ ] Run complete descaling cycle with citric acid solution (Quarterly)
- [ ] Replace water filter in reservoir (Every 2 months)

> [!WARNING]
> Always purge steam wand immediately before and after steaming milk to avoid milk siphoning back into the boiler.
