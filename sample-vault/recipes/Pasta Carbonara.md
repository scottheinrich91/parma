# Authentic Roman Spaghetti alla Carbonara

> [!WARNING]
> Under no circumstances should cream, peas, garlic, or onions be added to a classic Roman carbonara. The velvety sauce is a pure emulsion of egg yolks, pecorino cheese, and starchy pasta water.

---

## 🧀 Ingredients (Serves 2)

- **Spaghetti or Rigatoni**: 220g (bronze-die extruded pasta)
- **Guanciale (Cured Pork Jowl)**: 120g, cut into 1/4-inch thick lardons
- **Pecorino Romano D.O.P.**: 60g, finely microplaned (plus extra for serving)
- **Egg Yolks**: 4 large farm-fresh egg yolks + 1 whole egg
- **Black Peppercorns**: 2 teaspoons, freshly whole toasted and coarsely crushed in a mortar

---

## 🍳 Cooking Steps

1. **Crisp the Guanciale**: Place lardons in a cold skillet over medium-low heat. Slowly render the fat until the exterior is golden-crisp but the interior remains chewy (approx 8-10 mins). Remove pork and reserve the rendered liquid fat.
2. **Whisk the Carbocream**: In a heatproof bowl, vigorously whisk egg yolks, whole egg, grated Pecorino Romano, black pepper, and 2 tablespoons of warm rendered guanciale fat into a thick paste.
3. **Boil Pasta**: Cook pasta in moderately salted water (use half the normal salt since Guanciale & Pecorino are very salty) until 2 minutes shy of al dente.
4. **Emulsify**: Transfer hot pasta directly into the pan with remaining fat and 1/3 cup starchy pasta cooking water. Remove pan COMPLETELY from direct burner heat. Pour in egg mixture and toss vigorously for 60 seconds until a creamy emulsion forms.
5. **Serve**: Fold in crispy guanciale, top with freshly cracked black pepper and extra grated Pecorino.
