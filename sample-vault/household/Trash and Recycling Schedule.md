# ♻️ Waste, Recycling & Compost Schedule

> [!NOTE]
> **Pickup Day**: Every **Tuesday Morning** (Borders placed curbside by 7:00 AM)

---

## 📅 Weekly Bin Protocol

- 🟢 **Green Bin (Compost & Yard Waste)**: Every Tuesday (food scraps, coffee grounds from [[appliances/Espresso Machine]], yard trimmings).
- 🔵 **Blue Bin (Single-Stream Recycling)**: Every Tuesday (clean cardboard, aluminum cans, rigid plastic #1 & #2).
- ⚫ **Black Bin (Landfill Trash)**: Every other Tuesday.

---

## 🛢️ Hazardous & Electronic Waste
- Old lithium batteries, motor oil, and paint cans (from [[household/Paint Colors & Materials]]) must be dropped off at the County Eco-Depot on Saturdays (8am - 2pm).
