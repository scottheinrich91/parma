## 🚰 Emergency Shut-Off Locations

### Main Water Shut-Off
- **Location**: Front basement wall, near the water meter.
- **Action**: Turn the yellow ball-valve lever 90 degrees until perpendicular to the brass pipe.

### Electrical Main Breaker
- **Location**: Basement hallway next to the workbench.
- **Action**: Flip the large double 200A breaker at the very top of the panel down.

### Main Natural Gas Shut-Off
- **Location**: Outside east wall next to the gas meter.
- **Tool Needed**: Adjustable crescent wrench stored inside utility closet.
