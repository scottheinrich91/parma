# HVAC & Heat Pump Guide

> [!NOTE]
> **System**: Mitsubishi Diamond Comfort Heat Pump & Air Handler  
> **Filter Size**: `16" x 25" x 1"` (MERV 11 recommended)  
> **Thermostat**: Ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium

---

## 🔄 Filter Replacement Protocol

Replace return air filters every **90 days** (or every **60 days** during peak summer/winter months).

- **Filter Slot Location**: Basement ceiling directly beneath the main return air plenum.
- **Airflow Direction**: Ensure the arrow printed on the filter frame points **UP** toward the blower unit.
- **Recommended Brand**: FilterBuy MERV 11 Allergen Defense or Nordic Pure.

> [!WARNING]
> Do NOT use MERV 13+ dense fiberglass filters on this system, as excessive static pressure will cause the blower motor to overheat.

---

## 🌡️ Seasonal Operations

### Summer (Cooling)
- Set target temperature: 72°F – 74°F.
- Fan mode: `Auto` (keeps humidity low by allowing condensation to drain off coils).

### Winter (Heating)
- Set target temperature: 68°F – 70°F.
- Aux Heat threshold: -5°F outdoor ambient temperature.
