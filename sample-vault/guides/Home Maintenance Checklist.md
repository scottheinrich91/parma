# 🛠️ Home Maintenance Seasonal Checklist

> [!NOTE]
> Systematic preventive maintenance prevents expensive emergencies. Refer to the specific appliance guides when performing repairs.

---

## 🌸 Spring Checklist
- [ ] Inspect [[appliances/HVAC & Heat Pump|Heat Pump outdoor condenser]] and clear winter foliage.
- [ ] Flush sediment from water heater drain valve.
- [ ] Test all sump pumps and emergency battery backup systems.
- [ ] Inspect roof shingles and clean gutter downspouts.

## ☀️ Summer Checklist
- [ ] Deep clean [[appliances/Espresso Machine|Espresso Machine grouphead shower screen and solenoid valve]].
- [ ] Clean [[appliances/Dishwasher|Dishwasher filter basket and spray arms]].
- [ ] Touch up exterior trim paint using [[household/Paint Colors & Materials|Benjamin Moore formulas]].

## 🍂 Autumn Checklist
- [ ] Replace HVAC air filters (`16x25x1 MERV 11`).
- [ ] Disconnect and drain exterior garden hose bibs before first freeze.
- [ ] Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors in every bedroom.
- [ ] Verify emergency contact sheet is up to date ([[household/Emergency Contacts]]).

## ❄️ Winter Checklist
- [ ] Verify insulation wrap on exposed basement pipes near main shut-off valve.
- [ ] Inspect weatherstripping around exterior entry doors.
